About Us

Monroe's Home for Golf

KJ's Driving Range has been serving the Monroe community with a welcoming, well-kept facility for golfers of every skill level. From beginners picking up a club for the first time to competitive players dialing in their game, we provide the space and support to help everyone improve.

Our range is designed to be accessible, enjoyable, and affordable — because we believe great golf shouldn't be reserved for a select few. Come out, hit some balls, and see why locals keep coming back.
